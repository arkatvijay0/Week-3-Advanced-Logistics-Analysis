# Visualizations

This folder contains the visualizations generated during the Week 3 logistics data analysis.

## Visualizations Included

### 01_distributions.png

Distribution of numerical logistics variables using histograms.

**Purpose:** To understand the distribution, spread, and variability of the numerical data.

### 02_correlation_heatmap.png

Correlation heatmap showing relationships between numerical logistics variables.

**Purpose:** To identify strong positive and negative relationships between variables.

### 03_strongest_relationship.png

Scatter plot showing the strongest numerical relationship identified during the analysis.

**Purpose:** To visually examine the relationship between two highly correlated variables.

### 04_daily_shipment_trend.png

Line chart showing daily shipment volume.

**Purpose:** To identify shipment-volume fluctuations and changes over time.

### 05_outlier_analysis.png

Box plots showing potential outliers in numerical logistics variables.

**Purpose:** To identify unusually high or low observations that may require further investigation.

## Tools Used

* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Pandas
* Python
